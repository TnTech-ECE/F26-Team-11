## Problem:

The problem Team 11 shall solve is creating a cost-effective, portable electric

generator that effectively charges from a variety of sources, namely renewable energy. Our target customer is any entity in need of an electric generator that can be transported entirely by a single person without much effort. A crucial part of the project is for it to have the ability to power a broad selection of devices in remote locations with renewable energy, primarily solar power. Some specific use case examples are disaster aid and relief, technology demonstrations for outdoor environments, and dispersed camping.

Team 11 strongly believes that many of the existing solar powered generators are

not cost effective and that our project will match performance and safety for a lower cost.

## Background:

Generators throughout the years have been a staple in portable power, being able to

provide power to those off the grid. Combustion engine generators require fuel to operate which complicates transport and handling for a single person. Combustion generators also create constant noise and air pollution while in use. As a result, limiting the product to solely outdoor usage. Renewable energy generators aim to diminish these issues by removing the need for consumable fuel which improves air quality and having much quieter operation. Allowing store energy to be used indoors without long power cables. Targeting single person portability, a comparable combustion generator such as the WEN 2000 Watt Inverter creates noise pollution of around 53dB and requires transportation of liquid fuel to have sustained operation over multiple days. [1] A comparable renewable energy generator system such as the Jackery Solar Generator 2000 v2 costs over \$1000, making it non-viable for a considerable portion of consumers. [2] Transporting solar panels is significantly safer than transporting liquid fuel.

## Specification and constraints:

- The generator shall switch seamlessly between running on renewable energy and battery power if the voltage drops below a minimum threshold.


- The generator shall feature an embedded display displaying the power draw, power source at that moment, and any other important data as chosen by team 11 and the customer.

- The generator shall have a minimum runtime of 2 hours at full load and must output a minimum of 200 Watts during that time.

- The generator shall be made to be weatherproof such that it will operate at normal levels during typical weather patterns such as light to medium rain, cold, or high heat.

- The generator shall have adequate heat management for all heat-producing elements such as the battery so that the system does not overheat even in warmer outdoor conditions.

## Survey of Existing Solutions:

There are several existing solutions from DIY builds to fully commercialized products of renewable portable generators. Two particularly informative examples for industrialized solutions include the WEN 56203i 2000-Watt Inverter Generator and the Jackery Solar Generator 2000 v2. Although, these products represent different approaches when it comes to portable power generation. The WEN generator demonstrates conventional inverter-based AC generation, while on the contrary, the Jackery system integrates battery storage, solar charging, and multi-mode power delivery. Team 11 will use these existing products as useful ideas and design references when constructing our own generator.

## WEN 2000-Watt Generator

## Pros

- Affordability: Current manufacturer price is listed at \$499.99 which is under our cost budget at \$1000.

- Portability: Lightweight design makes it easy for portability for a single person. Approximately 39 lbs in total.

- Higher Power Output: Provides 1,700 W rated power and 2,000 W surge power which is substantially greater than our 200 W minimum requirement

## Cons

- Engine Noise and Heat: Even though it is quiet still produces mechanical noise and heat exhaust that we can avoid when designing

- Non- Renewable: Uses gasoline-powered combustion engine rather than renewable energy

- No Rechargeable Battery: Battery does not provide a battery storage system


- No Solar Charging: Does not use direct support for photoelectric charging.

## Jackery Solar Generator 2000 v2

## Pros

- Renewable: Compatible with photoelectric solar panels

- LiFePO4 Battery: Long life cycle with Jackery doing 4,000 cycles to at least 70% capacity.

- Large Battery Capacity: Contains 2,042 Wh. LiFeBO4 battery providing more power than our team’s minimum requirement of 400 Wh. (200 W x 2 hr.)

- High AC output: Provides 2,200 W continuous and 4,400 W surge output.

- Quiet Operation: Way quieter than a combustion engine generator

- Solar charging: Supports up to 400 W of solar input

- BMS / Battery protection: Provides the battery management required for Team 11

- USB-A & USB-C Outputs: Provides multiple low-voltage output for electronics.

- DC output: Provides 12 V DC

## Cons

- Expensive: Current manufacturer price is a bit above our cost budget of \$1000 listed at \$1,184.

## Measures of Success:

Measures of success would include meeting all the requirements laid out before

and describing methods to test them to ensure that the project meets or exceeds the goals.

## The generator will be tested on:

- Run time by running it at a specific load and ensuring that it runs for a minimum of two hours.

- Consistent power delivery by ensuring it operates with an uninterrupted supply by changing the power source and ensuring the change between sources is seamless.

- Portability by ensuring that it meets the team-s weight standards of being no higher than fifty pounds and has a means of simple transportation.

- Weather resistance by means of spraying the case with water and testing the water contents of the inside of the case to see the resistance rating.


- Affordability by comparing the cost of the project to ensure that it is no more than the maximum budget that team sets for the whole project, with the goal being to spend less than the closest competitor on the market.

- Usability by ensuring that the integrated display is readable, shows the battery level, shows the voltage levels, as well as any other decided upon information.

Specific Implications:

This project will provide an immediate solution to the customer’s problem of having

reliable and easily accessible power while hosting events or working in areas where there is not a readily available power source. During events where there are displays and interactive demonstrations, there is a need for reliable power to maintain the different needs at the event and depending on where the events are held that could be difficult to find. With the renewable energy generator, it will provide a power source to alleviate that needs through solar power or the pre-charged internal battery. This will enable events to go smoother, allowing for less trouble for the organizers and other people that may be in attendance.
