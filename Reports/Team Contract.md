## Introduction

## Team Expectations

In this section, the team will discuss and clearly define their collective expectations in various key areas. Be specific to ensure mutual understanding.

### 1. Participation (Engagement and Effort in the Project)

- **Expectation:** Each team member is expected to: o Actively participate in all team meetings and discussions. o Engage fully in project activities and contribute ideas. o Notify the team in advance if unable to attend a meeting or meet an
obligation.

### 2. Contribution (Progress on the Project)

- **Expectation:** Each team member is expected to: o Complete assigned tasks diligently and on time. o Contribute equal effort to the project's progress. o Share expertise and assist others when needed.

### 3. Communication

- **Expectation:** Team communication should be: o Professional, respectful, and considerate. o Conducted through agreed-upon channels. Primarily through a personal cell
phone group chat, but alternatively contacting through university provided email. o Documentation will be shared via email and git hub as appropriate. o Responsive, with replies to inquiries within 24 hours on weekdays.

### 4. Meetings

- **Expectation:** Regarding team meetings: o **Frequency:** The team will meet at least two of three selected days each
week depending on scheduling. These days are Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. From 1 PM to 3 PM; Targeting four hours weekly. Additionally, the team will utilize the reserved class period for the day that Team 11 is not assigned to attend ECE 4961. o **Attendance:** Members are expected to attend all meetings; absences should be communicated in advance. o **Preparation:** Members should come prepared, having completed any agreed-upon tasks or research.

### 5. Conduct

- **Expectation:** Team members will: o Treat each other with respect and courtesy. o Listen actively and allow others to express their ideas without interruption. o Address disagreements professionally and constructively.
### 6. Conflict Resolution

- **Expectation:** The team will: o Address conflicts promptly through open dialogue. o Use a step-by-step approach to resolve issues:
- Discuss the issue privately between involved parties.
- If unresolved, bring the issue to the team for discussion.
- Seek input from the instructor if necessary.

o Focus on finding mutually acceptable solutions.

### 7. Deadlines

- **Expectation:** Regarding deadlines: o All team-set and instructor-set deadlines are to be met. o If a member anticipates difficulty meeting a deadline, they must inform the
team as soon as possible. o The team will collaboratively adjust workloads to ensure project timelines are maintained.
